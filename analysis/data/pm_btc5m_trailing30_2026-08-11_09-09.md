# Polymarket BTC 5m, trailing 30 days (2026-08-11 to 2026-09-09): taker-side window statistics

Every taker fill on every BTC 5-minute up/down market for 30 consecutive UTC days: 54,746 wallets, $225.0M of
taker premium, $7.50M a day. Settlement PnL per wallet is gross (no fees were charged, no rewards, positions
settle within the day). Machines and people follow the combined rule: 300+ fills in a day, or 30+ fills with no
6-hour gap, or both outcomes bought in 40%+ of windows, or a dense share-typed run of 4h+. This supersedes the
sampled-day figures in `pm_btc5m_wallet_profitability_2026-09-10.md`. The seven-cohort grid over the same
window needs the maker legs, which are still downloading. Daily series: `btc5m_trailing30_daily.csv`.
Scripts: `scripts/pm_pull_btc5m_day.py`, `scripts/pm_trailing30.py`.

## Machines and people, day by day

Machine share of taker dollars: mean 67.1%, lowest 58.1% (Aug 15, a Saturday), highest 73.0% (Aug 25),
dollar-weighted 67.5%. Machines had positive settlement PnL on all 30 days (+0.30% to +2.67% per dollar);
people had negative PnL on all 30 days (-0.40% to -3.78%). The share of wallets ending a day ahead ranged
from 40.9% to 50.3%.

Assigning each wallet one class for the whole window (the class it held on most of its active days) gives
machines 2,704 wallets, 57.6% of dollars, PnL +1.54%, 40.8% of them profitable over the month; people 52,042
wallets, 42.4% of dollars, PnL -1.75%, 31.4% profitable. The gap between 67.5% (daily) and 57.6% (modal) is
wallets that run as machines on their busy days and trade by hand on quiet ones; their dollars sit mostly
on the machine days.

## Who is profitable over a month

Over the 30 days, 31.9% of wallets ended ahead and the median wallet lost 5.7% of what it traded. Single
days look like a coin flip (43 to 50% ahead); a month does not.

Gains are concentrated: the top 1% of wallets (547) took 81% of all gross gains over the month and the top
0.1% took half. Twenty-six wallets traded more than $1M each, 27% of all taker dollars, and 21 of them
ended ahead.

## Reading

- The month-long view removes the coin-flip: two thirds of wallets lose, the median loses 5.7%, and the
  losers are the occasional and the small. Regulars who trade 20 or more days out of 30 are 5% of wallets,
  60% of dollars, and about break even as a group; the 26 largest earn.
- Retail cadence is 3.3% of dollars over a full month, the same as on any single day. The 10-to-100 band, a
  third of dollars, loses 1.3% per dollar; the fast band, 62% of dollars, earns 1.2%. Cadence orders
  profitability the way the segmentation grid assumes.
- Machine share did not drift within the month: 58 to 73% by day, no trend, with weekends at the low end.
