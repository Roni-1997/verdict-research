## Method / caveats
- Kalshi: `GET /trade-api/v2/markets` (status=settled, series_ticker) only serves markets settled after ~Jun 25 2026; older markets come from `GET /trade-api/v2/historical/markets` (newest-first cursor walk per series back to Mar 1). Merged and de-duplicated by ticker; `volume_fp` per market, month = `close_time`.
- Kalshi "hourly above/below strike ladder" = series KXBTCD / KXETHD (all strikes, ~188 BTC strikes per hourly event in Aug). "Hourly range buckets" = series KXBTC (all its tickers; the few T-type tail markets inside it are <2% of its volume). Every Kalshi hourly/15m series shows a ~4-hour gap each Thursday (venue maintenance), so 84 of 96 fifteen-minute windows and 20 of 24 hourly events settle on Thursdays.
- Polymarket: `GET gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?series_id=…&end_date_min/max` weekly windows, deduped by event id, month = `endDate`. Series ids: BTC 5m 10684, BTC 15m 10192, BTC hourly 10114, BTC 4h 10331, BTC daily 41, BTC daily close ladder 45, BTC hit-price daily/weekly/monthly 10200/10151/10016, ETH 5m 10683, ETH 15m 10191, ETH hourly 10117, ETH daily close ladder 42, ETH hit-price monthly 10017, SOL 5m/15m 10686/10423, XRP 5m/15m 10685/10422.
- Weekly/monthly touch ladders are booked to the month they settle (their volume accrues over the life of the event).
- Kalshi contract counts overstate dollar premium ~3.6× versus Polymarket's premium figures; do not compare the two columns without the ×0.276 conversion.
